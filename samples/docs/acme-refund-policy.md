# Acme Learning – Refund Policy

_Last updated: 1 October 2026. This is a fictional sample document for the DocPilot demo._

## Self-paced courses

Learners can request a full refund within **14 days** of purchase, provided they have completed less than 30% of the course content. After 14 days, or once more than 30% of the content is completed, self-paced courses are non-refundable.

## Live bootcamps

Bootcamp fees are fully refundable until the first live session starts. After the first live session, learners may withdraw before the third session and receive a **50% refund**. No refunds are issued after the third live session.

If Acme Learning cancels or reschedules a bootcamp by more than 14 days, learners can choose a full refund or a free transfer to the next batch.

## How to request a refund

Email **refunds@acmelearning.example** from your registered email address with your order ID and the reason for the refund. Requests are reviewed within 2 business days.

## Processing time

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method. The amount usually reaches your bank account within **7 business days**. UPI and wallet refunds are typically faster (2–3 business days).

## EMI and loan-financed purchases

For purchases financed through a lending partner, the refund is paid to the lending partner, who closes or adjusts the loan. Any interest already charged by the partner is not refundable by Acme Learning.

## Exceptions

Certificates already issued, mock-interview credits that have been used, and personalised mentorship sessions that have already taken place are non-refundable.
