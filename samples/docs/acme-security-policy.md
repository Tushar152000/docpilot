# Acme Learning – Information Security Policy

_A fictional sample document for the DocPilot demo._

## Passwords and authentication

Passwords must be at least 12 characters long and must not be reused across services. Use the company password manager to generate and store passwords. Two-factor authentication (2FA) is **mandatory** for Google Workspace, Slack, GitHub and the payments dashboard.

## Handling student data

Student personal data (names, phone numbers, emails, payment details, assessment results) is confidential. Never share student data over personal WhatsApp, personal email or public links. Use only approved company systems, and share the minimum data needed for the task. Exports of student data require approval from the data protection lead.

## Devices

Company laptops must have full-disk encryption enabled and must lock automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity. Install software only from approved sources. Lost or stolen devices must be reported immediately.

## Reporting security incidents

Report any suspected security incident (phishing email, leaked credentials, lost laptop, unusual account activity) to **security@acmelearning.example** or the #security Slack channel within **1 hour** of noticing it. Do not try to investigate on your own and do not delete evidence.

## Access reviews

Managers review their team's access to systems every quarter. Access is removed within 24 hours when an employee leaves the company or changes role.

## AI tools

Do not paste confidential company or student data into public AI tools. Approved internal AI tools that keep data within the company account may be used for work.
