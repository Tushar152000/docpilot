# Acme Learning – Mentor & Employee Onboarding Guide

_A fictional sample document for the DocPilot demo._

## Your first week

Day 1: Collect your laptop from the IT desk, activate your company Google account, and complete the security awareness training (about 45 minutes).

Day 2: Meet your buddy. Every new hire is paired with an onboarding buddy for the first 30 days. Your buddy answers day-to-day questions and helps you find your way around.

Day 3–4: Shadow two live classes and one mock interview to see how sessions are run. Mentors should also review the curriculum for their track (Backend, Full Stack, or Data).

Day 5: First 1:1 with your manager to agree on 30-60-90 day goals.

## Tools and access

We use Slack for chat, Google Workspace for email and documents, Linear for engineering tasks, and Zoom for live classes. For laptop problems, software installs or access requests, email **it-help@acmelearning.example** or post in the #it-help Slack channel. Access requests are usually resolved within one business day.

## Working hours and leave

Core collaboration hours are 11:00 AM to 5:00 PM IST. Employees receive 24 days of paid leave per year plus public holidays. Leave should be applied for in the HR portal at least 3 days in advance, except in emergencies.

## Payroll and expenses

Salaries are credited on the last working day of every month. Submit expense claims with receipts in the HR portal before the 20th of the month to be reimbursed in the same month's payroll. Internet reimbursement for remote employees is capped at ₹1,500 per month.

## Learning budget

Every employee has an annual learning budget of ₹30,000 for courses, books and conferences. Get your manager's approval before spending it.
